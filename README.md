# sales-dashboard-superstore

## Overview
A sales analysis project using the Superstore dataset, built in Google Sheets. 
This project explores sales performance across product categories and regions 
to identify trends and business opportunities.

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets (Pivot Tables, Charts)

## Key Findings
1. Technology is the top-selling category, generating $827,455 in sales — 
   ahead of Furniture ($728,658) and Office Supplies ($705,422).
2. The West region is the strongest performer overall ($710,219 in sales), 
   while the South region consistently underperforms across every category.
3. East region leads specifically in Technology sales ($263,116), 
   suggesting strong demand for tech products in that area.

## Recommendation
Investigate why the South region lags behind — this could point to fewer 
stores, less marketing, or lower demand that leadership may want to address.

## Dataset
Superstore Sales dataset (public dataset, available on Kaggle)
